Sources & controls

One scene unit is one metre. Buildings retain the original angled surfaces and photographic mapping from Japan’s official PLATEAU survey models. Original survey photographs cover available façades and roofs. Terrain uses GSI elevation and aerial imagery, with native 20 cm Tokyo orthophotography in available Tama and Izu areas. Unphotographed surfaces keep their source colours; image resolution and survey dates vary.

All 62 Tokyo municipality datasets are connected. Nearby data streams as you walk. The local site requires internet for government source data.

Roads and bridges retain continuous survey surfaces. Smooth terrain uses approximately 2 m samples nearby, with coarser distant detail and shared edges. Survey dates and model detail vary. Roofs and footprints follow the source models; small features and dynamic street life are outside this dataset. Selected Shibuya interiors and street details are modeled in Blender. Authored interiors are identified in their room information. Physical bridge and elevated-road coverage is less complete than building coverage.
