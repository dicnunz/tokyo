Inside Shibuya

Furniture, fittings and surfaces are modeled in Blender at real-world scale. These are authored interior layouts connected to the Tokyo exterior.

Doors, furniture and walking paths have physical depth and collision. The café, clothing shop and apartment are interpretations of their building uses. Their layouts do not represent a measured survey of an actual shop or home.

Materials include Poly Haven CC0 surface scans. Open the café machine, doors and light switches with E when their prompt appears.
